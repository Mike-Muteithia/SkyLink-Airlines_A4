About SkyLink Airlines

SkyLink Airlines is a premier regional carrier dedicated to connecting cities with safety, reliability, and comfort. We believe that air travel should be accessible, efficient, and enjoyable for all our passengers.

Our Mission

To provide seamless and affordable flight experiences while maintaining the highest standards of safety and customer service.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted airline, fostering economic growth and personal connections through robust aviation networks.

Services & Destinations

  • Scheduled passenger flights across major regional hubs.
  • Economy, Business, and First Class travel options.
  • Reliable baggage handling and exceptional in-flight service.

Currently serving: Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, and Malindi.
